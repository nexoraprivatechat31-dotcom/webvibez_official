## 2. The 8 Non-Negotiable Modules for Modern Coaching Centers

A dedicated coaching management application must replace manual friction across every department. Here are the 8 foundational modules:

### Module 1: Student & Parent Mobile Portal
- Dedicated Android application published under **your institute's brand name** on the Google Play Store.
- Clean dashboard displaying daily timetables, upcoming test schedules, attendance percentages, and direct announcements.
- Eliminates messy WhatsApp groups where student queries get buried.

### Module 2: Smart Attendance & Instant Parent Notifications
- Fast QR code scan or teacher roll-call interface (< 1.5 seconds per student check-in).
- Automated SMS and WhatsApp alerts sent to parents immediately upon check-in or unexcused absence.
- Protects student safety and eliminates daily attendance telephone inquiries from concerned parents.

### Module 3: Computerized CBT Examination Engine (JEE/NEET UI)
- Accurate NTA-standard computerized testing interface.
- Includes question status palette (Answered, Marked for Review, Not Visited), sectional time counters, and automated negative marking calculations.
- Instant score delivery and sub-topic breakdown immediately after test submission.

### Module 4: Diagnostic Performance Radars & Scorecards
- Visual radar analytics highlighting student mastery across individual topics (e.g., Organic Chemistry vs Mechanics).
- Historic rank trajectory curves tracking student improvement over successive mock exams.
- 1-click printable PDF progress report cards for parent-teacher meetings.

### Module 5: Adaptive Bitrate Live Streaming & Class Archival
- Low-latency live classes for hybrid or remote student batches.
- In-class student doubt queue moderated by faculty.
- Automated lecture recording archiving directly into the student study vault for 24/7 revision.

### Module 6: Anti-Piracy Study Material Vault
- Digital distribution for DPPs (Daily Practice Problems), formula sheets, and handwritten lecture notes.
- **Dynamic Watermarking**: The student's verified registered mobile number and unique session timestamp are dynamically rendered as a semi-transparent watermark across all PDF pages.
- **Native Screen Capture Blackout**: Enforced using Android's native `FLAG_SECURE` window attribute to prevent unauthorized screenshots and screen recording.

### Module 7: 0% Commission Direct UPI Fee Collection
- Direct integration with institute UPI IDs and payment gateways.
- 100% of student fee settlements go directly into your coaching center's bank account without middleman escrow.
- Automated WhatsApp payment links and instant digital GST receipts sent upon transaction confirmation.

### Module 8: Multi-Branch Administrative ERP Cockpit
- Role-based permissions for Managing Directors, Branch Managers, Subject Teachers, and Accounts teams.
- Real-time aggregated telemetry covering batch revenue, faculty workload, student drop-off rates, and operational overhead.

## 3. Data Sovereignty: Who Truly Owns Your Student Directory?

Many institute directors overlook the fine print of generic aggregator platforms:
- **Lead Cannibalization**: Free or low-cost SaaS apps often market competitor courses, discount vouchers, or private tutoring directly to your enrolled students.
- **Platform Lock-In**: If you decide to leave an aggregator app, exporting your question banks, past test records, and parent communication history is intentionally made difficult.
- **Data Privacy Compliance**: Enrolled student phone numbers and parent contacts represent your academy's most valuable intellectual asset.
